shortstobench

shortstobench is a private desktop tool used only by its owner to upload and schedule short videos on the owner's own YouTube channels. It is not offered to the public.

What it does

  • Uploads videos that the owner made to the owner's YouTube channels and sets the scheduled publish time.
  • Reads back the uploaded video's title, privacy status and schedule to confirm the upload.

It uses the YouTube Data API with the youtube.force-ssl scope, only for the channels the owner signs in with.
